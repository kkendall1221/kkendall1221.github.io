Selected Reading

The books that
shaped how I think.

A working library, grouped by the questions it answers. Not a definitive list — a personal one, revised as I read.

Economics & Markets Business & Finance Philosophy & Ideas Classics Advanced

Economics & Markets

02 titles
Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order
Dalio's study of the long historical cycles that move empires, currencies, and reserve-currency status — and a framework for where the U.S. and China sit in that arc today.
Ray Dalio
How Countries Go Broke: The Big Cycle
A close look at the "big debt cycle": how nations accumulate unsustainable debt, what national insolvency actually looks like, and how it ties into the wider shifts in the world order.
Ray Dalio

Business & Finance

05 titles
Your Next Five Moves
Strategy framed like chess — thinking several moves ahead about who you want to become, the team around you, and the blind spots that derail most plans.
Patrick Bet-David
Way of the Wolf
Belfort's "Straight Line" system distilled into a repeatable method for persuasion, influence, and closing.
Jordan Belfort
Money: Master the Game
Lessons drawn from interviews with some of the world's top investors, turned into a step-by-step plan for building financial security.
Tony Robbins
The 10X Rule
The case that most people badly underestimate the effort a goal demands — and the argument for setting targets, and action, an order of magnitude higher.
Grant Cardone
Dave Ramsey's Complete Guide to Money
The plain-language playbook behind Ramsey's debt-free philosophy: budgeting, getting out of debt, and building wealth on a clear set of rules.
Dave Ramsey

Philosophy & Ideas

04 titles
Atomic Habits
How tiny, compounding changes to your systems — rather than your goals — drive lasting behavior change.
James Clear
The Happiness Advantage
The research-backed argument that happiness fuels success, not the other way around, and how a positive baseline improves performance.
Shawn Achor
Switch On Your Brain
A neuroscientist's case that thought patterns physically reshape the brain — and a practical method for directing them.
Dr. Caroline Leaf
The Power of Your Subconscious Mind
A classic on how belief and the subconscious quietly shape the outcomes we experience.
Joseph Murphy

Classics

02 titles
Dracula
The 1897 epistolary novel that defined the modern vampire — still a masterclass in dread, structure, and atmosphere.
Bram Stoker
Think and Grow Rich
Hill's 1937 distillation of success principles, drawn from years studying the era's wealthiest industrialists.
Napoleon Hill

Advanced

02 titles
International Economics
A rigorous, current treatment of trade and international finance, grounded in real historical cases — from comparative advantage to currency crises.
Menzie D. Chinn & Douglas A. Irwin
The Econometrics of Financial Markets
The graduate-level standard for the statistical methods behind modern asset pricing — dense, technical, and foundational.
Campbell, Lo & MacKinlay
